#  About Me:

I am a Computer Science researcher with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Healthcare Informatics. Currently, I am pursuing an MSc in Computer Science & Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). I hold a BSc in Computer Science & Engineering from East Delta University, where I built a strong foundation in machine learning, data analytics, and academic research.

---

My research primarily revolves around AI applications in healthcare, focusing on early diagnosis of critical medical conditions, AI-driven disease detection, bioinformatics, and predictive analytics. I am particularly passionate about leveraging AI to enhance clinical decision-making and improve patient outcomes.

### Research Interests:
- Bioinformatics & Medical Imaging
- AI-Powered Disease Detection
- Clinical Data Analysis & Predictive Analytics
- Human-Computer Interaction in Healthcare
- Natural Language Processing & Large Language Models (NLP & LLMs)

---

With expertise in Python, PyTorch, and TensorFlow, I have contributed to multiple research projects and publications. I have authored and presented research papers at IEEE conferences, addressing early disease detection, clinical data interpretation, and AI applications in mental health. My works, including my undergraduate thesis, have been published in international journals and featured in prestigious academic forums.

Beyond research, I am skilled in data analysis, machine learning model development, and academic writing. My technical proficiencies extend to data visualization, scientific writing (LaTeX), and technical communication, complemented by collaborative teamwork and analytical problem-solving.

---

I am actively exploring research collaborations and industry partnerships to apply AI in solving real-world healthcare challenges. My objective is to harness cutting-edge deep learning techniques to enhance early diagnosis and intervention strategies.
Feel free to connect with me to discuss research collaborations, AI innovations, or the latest trends in deep learning!
